LESSON 2: OBJECTIVES
====================

After the core exercises, you should be able to:

  * Distinguish a byte, an address, a pointer, and a HANDLE.
  * Explain DB, EQU, LEA, and the size in QWORD PTR.
  * Map all five WriteFile arguments to registers and a stack slot.
  * Distinguish the BOOL return value from the bytes-written output.
  * Derive 38h for this frame and explain both RSP viewpoints.
  * Identify why a volatile register cannot hold live state across a call.

Completion: Tiers 1 and 2 in exercises.txt, including the byte-count
observation. The repeated-write extension is optional.
